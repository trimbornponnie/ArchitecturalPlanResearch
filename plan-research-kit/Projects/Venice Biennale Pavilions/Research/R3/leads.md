# R3 leads: French Pavilion (not downloaded, with exact URL and why it stopped)

## Archive leads (no online copy)
L1. ASAC (La Biennale di Venezia, Archivio Storico delle Arti Contemporanee, VEGA Marghera): files with plans of the
    French pavilion, from the Fondo storico inventories (rows r3-0001..r3-0007):
    - Scatole nere. Padiglioni b. 5 "Francia" fasc. 4 "Padiglione della Francia": planimetrie (1926-1928);
    - Paesi b. 12 "Francia" fasc. 1: n. 1 pianta del padiglione (1947-1949);
    - Lavori e gestione delle sedi, Padiglioni b. 5 fasc. 1: piante dei padiglioni, 1958 works (France among 22);
    - Lavori e gestione delle sedi, Padiglioni b. 7 fasc. 1 "Francia": n. 5 piante, nota sul padiglione, 1 foto
      (1951-1955) -- the best pointer for the building itself;
    - Lavori e gestione delle sedi, Padiglioni b. 9 fasc. 12 "Lavori al Padiglione Francia": ristrutturazione 1975;
    - Architettura series, agibilità files with plans: b. 291/3 (2000), 341 (2002), 416/3 and 423/17 (2006), 436
      (2008), 456 (2010), 469 (2012), 490 fasc. 3 (2018 progetti dell'allestimento).
    Contact route: ASAC research room (asac.labiennale.org), request scans of the named files.

## Print / lending leads
L3. Sophie Calle, "Take care of yourself", Actes Sud 2007 (French pavilion 2007): archive.org item
    https://archive.org/details/takecareofyourse0000call is lending-only (inlibrary, access-restricted); not downloaded.
    Unlikely to hold a pavilion plan; listed for completeness.

L4. Comune di Venezia, Archivio storico comunale (Celestia) / Ufficio tecnico: the 1912 pavilion was designed by the
    municipal chief engineer Faust Finzi (Wikipedia/areq snippets, unconfirmed); construction drawings 1911-1912 would
    sit in the municipal technical office files. Contact route: Archivio Storico del Comune di Venezia, Celestia;
    ask for the "Padiglione della Francia ai Giardini, 1911-1912" project (Finzi, Ufficio tecnico comunale).
L5. Print: Marco Mulazzani, "I padiglioni della Biennale di Venezia" (Electa 1988, 2004, 2014): chapter on the French
    pavilion, usually with plan and elevation. A. Re Rebaudengo (ed.), "Pavilions and Garden of Venice Biennale"
    (Contrasto 2013): France chapter. Library copies (IUAV, BNM Venezia); no open scan known.
L6. 1990 competition for the French pavilion (curator Jean-Louis Froment): proposals by Christian de Portzamparc
    and Philippe Starck (Nouvel's on jeannouvel.com, rows r3-0011..0016). Exhibition catalogue of the French pavilion
    1990 (AFAA) not traced; Portzamparc's site has no page for it (sitemap read); starck.com closed (hand H4).
L7. 2025-2026 renovation of the pavilion by the French Embassy in Italy (culture.gouv.fr 2025 press release): the
    architect and the tender are not traced; tender page closed by robots (hand H2). The renovation set (survey and
    project drawings, likely DWG/PDF) is the best modern source; contact route: Ambassade de France en Italie, service
    immobilier / Institut français (Pavillon français).
