What could not be downloaded, where it is and why it stopped. R4 leads L1-L11 stay valid; items here are new or updated.

## N1. ASAC Fototeca: "Padiglione Gran Bretagna" photo dossier with two plans (archive-only; row r104-0001)
https://asac.labiennale.org/collezioni/fototeca/fascicoli/538597 : SEDI, collocazione PAD.STRANIERI.2.111-131, 1909-1996,
18 photos + 3 slides (Naya, Ferruzzi, Giacomelli); nr. 130 and nr. 131 "s.d. - Pianta". No digitised image online
(checked 2026-10-10). Route: ASAC, Via delle Industrie 23/9, Porto Marghera (reading room / reproduction request by
collocazione PAD.STRANIERI.2.130 and .131). Stopped: archive-only.

## N2. ASAC Fondo storico, serie Architettura: Gran Bretagna participation and "agibilita" files with plans (row r104-0002)
Inventory https://asac.labiennale.org/pdf/fondo-storico/04-Architettura.pdf (on disk): b. 291/3 (2000, planimetrie),
b. 335 and b. 375 (2002), b. 423/17 (2006 agibilita: planimetrie, relazioni tecnico-illustrative), b. 457 (2010
agibilita: piante, relazioni tecniche), 2018 unit on inventory p. 217. Exhibition-period safety plans of the whole
pavilion (likely incl. basement). Stopped: archive-only.

## N3. "Britain at the Venice Biennale 1895-1995" (British Council 1995): Internet Archive copy is lending-only
https://archive.org/details/britainatveniceb0000unse : access-restricted-item true (inlibrary, printdisabled), 170
images. Controlled lending may not be used (rule 2). Stopped: library copy needed (ISBN 978-0-86355-283-0).
Replaces R4 H6 (no public download exists).

## N4. "Le muse inquiete. La Biennale di Venezia di fronte alla storia" (La Biennale di Venezia, 2020), section
"I Padiglioni dei Giardini / Giardini's Pavillons" (pp. ~380-420?): pavilion-by-pavilion entries with ASAC photos
(V. Pajusco wrote the Central Pavilion entry, pp. 392-393, open on iris.unive.it hdl 10278/3734099). Whether a British
pavilion entry with plans exists is open. Stopped: library (catalogue), no open copy of other entries found.

/api. Theses and papers on the pavilion therefore not searched in these two repositories.
