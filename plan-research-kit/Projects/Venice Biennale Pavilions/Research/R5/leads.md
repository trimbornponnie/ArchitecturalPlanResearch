# R5 leads: United States Pavilion

## Archive-only (originals not online)

1. ASAC, Archivio Storico delle Arti Contemporanee, La Biennale di Venezia (Fondo storico; inventories read locally from
   the PDFs R2 fetched: https://asac.labiennale.org/pdf/fondo-storico/04-Scatole_nere_padiglioni.pdf ,
   .../04-Paesi.pdf , .../05-Lavori_e_gestione_delle_sedi.pdf). Plan files of the US pavilion:
   - Scatole nere padiglioni b. 15 "Stati Uniti e America latina" 1919-1939 (inventory p. 11): fasc. 1 "Fotografie del
     padiglione"; fasc. 9 "Padiglione americano 1928": planimetrie, ritagli, corrispondenza (1929-1931) = the design /
     construction years; fasc. 10 "Padiglione degli Stati Uniti d'America 1930": opuscoli a stampa; fasc. 11 "XXI Biennale
     1938": planimetrie; fasc. 13 "Stati Uniti d'America": planimetrie (1933-1934).
   - Paesi b. 26 "Stati Uniti d'America" 1929-1955 (inventory pp. 67-68): 1948 fasc. 1a "Atti relativi alla costruzione
     del padiglione degli Stati Uniti d'America per conto della Grand Central Art Galleries di New York - agosto 1929"
     (contract copy 10.12.1954 for the Demanio Comunale site, correspondence on demolishing the cafe-restaurant on the
     site and building the new pavilion, 1929-1954); 1948 fasc. 1b sale of the pavilion by the Grand Central Art
     Galleries to the US government; 1950/1952 files on the purchase of the pavilion by the US government / sale to MoMA
     (1951-1955).
   - Lavori e gestione delle sedi, Padiglioni: b. 1 Padiglioni stranieri 1938-1957, fasc. 5 "Padiglione Stati Uniti
     d'America. 1940 e seguenti fino al 1957" (works, invoices, estimates); b. 5 Padiglioni stranieri 1956-1959, fasc. 1
     "Lavori ai padiglioni stranieri nel 1958": piante dei padiglioni incl. Stati Uniti; b. 7 Padiglioni stranieri /
     Padiglione Venezia 1935-1966, fascicoli nominativi: "Stati Uniti: quaderno rilegato contenente i dati delle
     partecipazioni 1930, n. 1 fotografia e n. 5 piante, nota sul padiglione".
   Route: ASAC reading room (Parco Scientifico Tecnologico VEGA, Marghera), request by signature; ask for scans of the
   1929-31 planimetrie (b. 15 fasc. 9), the 5 piante in Lavori b. 7 and the 1958 plan in Lavori b. 5.
2. Avery Drawings & Archives, Columbia University: "Delano & Aldrich architectural records and papers, 1900-1949"
   (approx. 7,000 drawings, 6 boxes of photographs), https://findingaids.library.columbia.edu/archives/cul-3460565 .
   Finding aid and EAD (https://findingaids.library.columbia.edu/ead/cul-3460565.xml, fetched) have no item list and do
   not name the Venice pavilion; whether drawings of it survive there is `open`. Contact avery-drawings@columbia.edu
   (appointment; ask whether the collection holds the 1929-30 US Pavilion, Venice, drawings). CLIO record
   https://clio.columbia.edu/catalog/3460565 closed to scripts (robots.txt).

## Print publications and other contacts

3. Marco Mulazzani, "I padiglioni della Biennale di Venezia" (Electa 1988, 2004, 2014): chapter on the US pavilion
   (Delano & Aldrich 1930), expected to reproduce the original plan/elevation from ASAC. Library copy (IUAV, BNM).
4. 1930 periodicals: en.wikipedia "17th Venice Biennale" (closed to the guard) cites "The Architect" (New York) 1930 on
   the pavilion; Pencil Points / American Architect / Architecture 1930 may also carry it. 1930 volumes are US public
   domain since 2026: HathiTrust (closed to the guard, hand.md), usmodernist.org library (no script-readable full-text
   search; https://www.usmodernist.org/library.htm lists the magazines).
5. Exhibition catalogues with installation drawings on the pavilion plan: OfficeUS (2014; "OfficeUS Atlas" / "OfficeUS
   Manual", Lars Mueller 2015-16); The Architectural Imagination (Log 37, 2016); Dimensions of Citizenship (2018,
   Inventory Press); American Framing (2021, Park Books; and Paul Preissner "Kind of Recent Projects and Drawings");
   Sketches on Everlasting Plastics (CBAC, 2023); PORCH (2025). Library copies.
6. Peggy Guggenheim Collection, Library / Archive / Photo Library (operator of the pavilion since 1986; remote services
   by e-mail, https://www.guggenheim-venice.it/en/art/library-archive-photography/): ask for survey plans of the
   pavilion and documentation of maintenance / restoration works since 1986.
7. MoMA Archives (owner 1954-1986; International Program records) and the Grand Central Art Galleries records
   (Archives of American Art, Smithsonian) - not searched online in this run; likely to hold 1930s photos and
   correspondence, possibly plan prints.
8. US Department of State, ECA Cultural Programs Division: NOFO packages for the US Pavilion (grants.gov, e.g.
   SFOP0008572 for 2023) may include a pavilion floor plan (hand.md).
