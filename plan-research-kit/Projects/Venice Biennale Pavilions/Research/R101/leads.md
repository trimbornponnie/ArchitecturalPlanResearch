# R101 leads (not downloaded): German Pavilion, gap pass for R1

R1's leads.md items 1-21 stay valid; the items below are new or updated by R101.

## Print / archive leads

1. **Zentralblatt der Bauverwaltung 58 (1938), Nr. 44, pp. 1192-1194** (R1 lead 1, still open). R101 checked: the
   ZLB "15849354" record family is the Berlin telephone directory, not the journal; Paderborn's PDF 8238864 is a 1932
   advert. No digital copy of the 1938 volume found. Ask TU Berlin UB / TIB Hannover / ZLB for a scan.
2. **Hugo von Habermann's sketches for the 1909 Bavarian pavilion** (fact from the 2022 catalogue p.123: Donghi built
   it "after sketches by Hugo von Habermann" of the Munich Secession). Possible holders: Muenchener Secession archive,
   Bayerisches Hauptstaatsarchiv (Staatsgemaeldesammlungen files, see R1 lead 15), Monacensia / Staedtische Galerie im
   Lenbachhaus. Would give the only design drawings of the original state.
3. **Arnold Bode model of the 1957 Umbauplan, realised by Liam Gillick 2009** (anodised aluminium, 26 x 30 x 12 cm,
   edition of 25; source https://2009.deutscher-pavillon.org/home.htm). Photos likely in Gillick's catalogue "Wie
   wuerden Sie sich verhalten? Eine Kuechenkatze spricht" (Sternberg Press 2009) - de-alt model.
4. **"this is modern" (Deutscher Werkbund Berlin, Venice 2014), the other 19 proposals.** Found online now: kleihues+
   kleihues with Heike Hanada (17 images, r101-0008..0024), ingenhoven (r1-0002, r101-0025), schneider+schumacher
   (r1-0001). Names of the remaining offices only in the catalogue (Claudia Kromrei (ed.), jovis 2014, EN ISBN
   978-3-86859-300-6 / DE 978-3-86859-283-2) and in the jovis press release
   http://www.jovis.de/media/pdf/DWA2014_Pressemitteilung_english.pdf (www.jovis.de did not resolve for the guard:
   DNS failure 2026-10-10). The exhibition photos on the ingenhoven page (WB-Venedig-008/009-A4.jpg) show the 22
   models in vitrines; a photo name lists Paul Kahlfeldt, Andreas Hild, Christoph Ingenhoven, Natalie Bottoli.
5. **ARCH+ 217 (2014), Bungalow Germania feature, fig. 4** "Schnitt und Grundriss der Montage aus Bungalow und
   Pavillon" with 10 m scale bar: only a small spread online (r101-0007). Print copy or CLA's office (Ciriacidis
   Lehnerer, see R1 hand.md 11) for the drawing at full size; CLA/ETH evidently hold a measured section and plan of the
   pavilion (2014).
6. **Bungalow Germania catalogue** (Hatje Cantz 2014): the 1432 px spreads are the largest the site serves
   (bungalow_germania_p28.jpg without "s": HTTP 404).
7. **cfk architetti, Venice (Clemens F. Kusch, Martin Weigert, Andrea Giovanni Zigon)**: architects of record of the
   pavilion (2006-2026, "Technical Management and Architectural Supervision"). They hold the survey CAD of the building.
   Their former domain http://www.cfk.it no longer resolves (guard: DNS failure). Contact via the German Pavilion
   commissioners (ifa, BBSR) - a request, not done here.
8. **a.k.a. ingenieure (Thomas Beck)**, engineers in the 2025 Stresstest team (PV roof on the listed building): roof
   and PV permit drawings (Soprintendenza Venezia) - lead.
9. **Arno Loebbecke** (architectural research and plans 2022, team page of 2022.deutscher-pavillon.org): CAD/DWG of the
   chronology drawings (R1 lead 15).
10. **Liam Gillick 2009 / Isa Genzken 2007 / Schlingensief 2011 exhibition plans**: no plans online; 2011 press kit on
    cdn.deutscher-pavillon.org (closed, hand.md 4).
11. **Making Heimat 2016 (DAM)**: dam-online.de has no page for it any more (exhibitions and archive pages checked);
    DAM archive (dam.archiv@stadt-frankfurt.de, listed on dam-online.de) may hold the exhibition drawings of the four
    wall openings.
