# R4 leads (British Pavilion)

What could not be downloaded and where it is.


## L1. ASAC, Archivio Storico della Biennale (fondo storico), archive-only

Inventories (online, saved locally): https://asac.labiennale.org/pdf/fondo-storico/04-Scatole_nere_padiglioni.pdf ,
https://asac.labiennale.org/pdf/fondo-storico/05-Lavori_e_gestione_delle_sedi.pdf ,
https://asac.labiennale.org/pdf/fondo-storico/04-Paesi.pdf . Files with plans of the British pavilion (no scans online):
- Scatole nere, b. 9/5 "XI Biennale 1914: padiglione della Gran Bretagna": planimetrie (1913-1915); b. 9/2 1909
  (opening of the British pavilion: printed brochure); b. 9/4 "Conti padiglione inglese dal 1909 al 1915" (accounts of
  the conversion; may name the works).
- Scatole nere, b. 8/6 "XX Biennale 1936: Gran Bretagna": corrispondenza, planimetrie (1935-1936).
- Lavori e gestione delle sedi, Padiglioni stranieri b. 5/1 (1956-1959): piante dei padiglioni, Gran Bretagna
  (with "Pignoramento del padiglione della Gran Bretagna" 1959 and the use convention).
- Lavori e gestione delle sedi, b. 7 "Padiglioni stranieri, Padiglione Venezia" (1935-1966): Gran Bretagna: bound
  notebook of participations 1909-1930, 1 photograph, 5 plans, 1 loose plan, 1954 convention, note on the pavilion.
Route: ASAC reading room (Parco Scientifico Vega, Marghera), request by segnatura; ask for scans of the 5+1 plans.

## L2. Brooks Murray Architects, 1993-95 refurbishment of the British Pavilion (survey and works drawings)

Page: https://www.brooksmurray.com/projects/british-pavilion-biennale/ (photos only online). The practice produced a
condition report and planned-maintenance schedule for the British Council and led a joint UK/Italian team to the
refurbishment completed early 1995. Its archive should hold measured survey plans/sections and works drawings
(basement, ground floor, roof). Contact: Brooks Murray Architects, 41 Tabernacle Street, London EC2A 4AA,
admin@brooksmurray.com. Also the British Council (estates) for the same set. The Italian partner is not named.

## L5. Italian heritage and municipal records

catalogo.beniculturali.it has no record of the pavilion (two searches, 2026-10-10). The pavilion stands on municipal
land (concession renewed 1954, see ASAC b. 7) and is a listed part of the Giardini; the Soprintendenza Archeologia,
Belle Arti e Paesaggio per il Comune di Venezia e Laguna and the Comune di Venezia (Edilizia privata / archivio
pratiche) should hold the 1993-95 refurbishment permit drawings (Brooks Murray with an Italian team) and the 2018
"Island" scaffold permit (planning consultant M+B studio s.r.l., structural engineer Zero4uno ingegneria). Route:
written request (accesso agli atti) by the owner's representative; not possible here.

## L7. Engineers, contractors and planners of "Island" 2018 (class 9; no web page found, no search budget left)

From the CSJ credits: structural engineer Zero4uno ingegneria s.r.l (scaffold and deck structure over the pavilion);
planning consultant M+B studio s.r.l (Venice permit drawings); main contractor Metalmontaggi s.r.l; scaffolding
Altrad Generation (UK) / Altrad DESSA, which "designed the plans for the Futuro to be erected" (scaffmag.com, 2018).
Their drawing sets would include surveyed plans/sections of the pavilion. Route: the firms' own websites (not
located) or a request to them; also Max Fordham LLP (services) and Turner & Townsend (cost).
