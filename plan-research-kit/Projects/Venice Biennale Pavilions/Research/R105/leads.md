# R105 leads: United States Pavilion (gap route for R5)

## Print publications (new, from en.wikipedia "American pavilion", Further reading)

1. Philip Rylands, Enzo Di Martino, "Flying the Flag for Art: The United States and the Venice Biennale 1895-1991"
   (Richmond, Wyldbore & Wolferstan, 1993; OCLC 901088904). The Peggy Guggenheim Collection's own history of the
   pavilion (Rylands was its director): most likely print source of the 1930 Delano & Aldrich plan and elevation.
   Library copy; Peggy Guggenheim Collection library (remote services by e-mail).
2. Annika Hossain, "Zwischen Kulturrepraesentation und Kunstmarkt: die USA bei der Venedig Biennale 1895-2015"
   (Emsdetten/Berlin, Edition Imorde, 2015; ISBN 978-3-942810-32-6). Dissertation; may reproduce ASAC plans. Library
   copy; check whether the university repository holds an open PDF (not found in this run, see notes).
3. Cristiana Volpi, "United States", in Adele Re Rebaudengo (ed.), "Pavilions and Garden of Venice Biennale" (Rome,
   Contrasto, 2013), p. 173 (ISBN 978-88-6965-440-4). Library copy.
