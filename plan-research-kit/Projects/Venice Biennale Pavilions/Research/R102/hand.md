## H1. RAAN (Russian Art Archive Network, Garage Museum): plan of the Russian pavilion 1996 and related sheets
Images sit on hb.bizmrg.com, whose robots.txt disallows every agent. Open each record page; use the page's own
viewer/download button (it may offer the TIFF original; access level "Available on request" may mean the TIFF needs a
request, in which case save the largest JPEG the viewer shows).
- https://russianartarchive.net/en/catalogue/document/D21933 "План Русского павильона в Венеции" (1996, 3 A4 sheets,
  Olga Tobreluts and Andrei Khaas Archive). Save as `ru_raan_D21933_1.jpg`, `_2`, `_3` (or `.tif`). Rows r102-0001..0003.
  1200 px variants seen in the HTML:
  - https://hb.bizmrg.com/raan/D/000021933/D21933/aa5d150f-5562-4a59-aec6-2aa6f4b77176-1200px.jpg
  - https://hb.bizmrg.com/raan/D/000021933/D21933/67424f0d-17b9-4b98-828a-b7df9e21fa16-1200px.jpg
  - https://hb.bizmrg.com/raan/D/000021933/D21933/ea16faf0-4dae-41d9-8c94-192e2f19ef4f-1200px.jpg
- https://russianartarchive.net/en/catalogue/document/D21935 (1998, "Экспозиция проекта «Новый Русский Классицизм»"):
  the verso of the last sheet has a hand-drawn plan. Save image 8 of 8 as `ru_raan_D21935_8.jpg`
  (https://hb.bizmrg.com/raan/D/000021935/D21935/a3ee3d0f-6790-4fe7-888e-f713f2c24dd4-1200px.jpg); look at all 8 and also
  save any other plan. Row r102-0004.
- https://russianartarchive.net/en/catalogue/document/D21931 (14 images) and D21943 (5 images), same archive, 1998-99
  exhibition project texts: save only sheets that show a plan of the pavilion, as `ru_raan_D21931_<n>.jpg`.
- 2014 Garage exhibition "The Russian Pavilion in Venice: Between Past and Future": booklets
  https://russianartarchive.net/en/catalogue/document/E3606 (2 images), E24185 (3 images) and press release E7933
  (7 images). Save any page that reproduces a Shchusev drawing or a plan as `ru_raan_<code>_<n>.jpg`.
- The 2014 book L33305 (https://russianartarchive.net/en/catalogue/document/L33305): only the cover is online; save it
  as `ru_raan_L33305_cover.jpg` if it shows a drawing (row r102-0005 is the book pointer).
