# R1 leads (not downloaded): German Pavilion

## Print / library leads

1. **"G.": Das deutsche Kunstausstellungsgebaeude in Venedig.** Zentralblatt der Bauverwaltung (vereinigt mit
   Zeitschrift fuer Bauwesen) 58 (1938), Nr. 44, pp. 1192-1194. Contemporary publication of Haiger's 1938 rebuilding;
   most likely source of a measured 1938 plan/section. ZLB Berlin digitised the Zentralblatt only to 1931 (search
   result); the 1931-44 merged title has a record at https://www.archivportal-d.de/item/RZIC77N4SITD6MPP47FMSQ5QZ4PC3SXD
   (bot wall, see hand.md). Ask: TU Berlin UB / ZLB / TIB Hannover for a scan of pp. 1192-1194.
2. **Ernst Haiger estate / 1938 drawings**: Haiger lived in Munich (to 1945) and Wiesbaden (d. 1952). Candidates:
   Architekturmuseum der TU Muenchen (collection online), Bundesarchiv (R 9361 file cited by Wikipedia is a party
   card, not drawings), Politisches Archiv des Auswaertigen Amts (the Reich owned the pavilion from 1938?), ASAC Venice.
3. **Daniele Donghi 1909 Padiglione Bavarese**: Donghi was a Venice municipal engineer (Ufficio tecnico comunale);
   drawings may sit in the Archivio Storico del Comune di Venezia (Celestia) and ASAC (fondo "padiglioni").
4. W. Ruediger, article on the 1938 Biennale in "Die Kunst" (Die Kunst fuer Alle) 1938; G. Marchiori in Emporium 1938
   (Emporium is digitised by the Scuola Normale / artgallery archives). Photos likely, plan possible.
5. Ursula Zeller, Elke aus dem Moore (eds.), Die deutschen Beitraege zur Biennale Venedig 1895-2007 / Germany's
   contributions to the Venice Biennale 1895-2007, DuMont 2007/2009, ISBN 978-3-8321-9249-5 (Becker essay pp. 63-88;
   historic photos of Padiglione Bavarese 1909/1912).
6. Verena Hartbaum, Stephan Trueby, Germania, Venezia. Die deutschen Beitraege zur Architekturbiennale Venedig seit
   1991, Wilhelm Fink 2016: documents every architecture exhibition in the pavilion and "how the building itself
   changed" (Stylepark 2016) -> exhibition plans likely.
7. Cristiana Volpi, "Germany", in A. Re Rebaudengo (ed.), Pavilions and Garden of Venice Biennale, Contrasto 2013,
   p. 182, ISBN 978-88-6965-440-4.
8. Bungalow Germania catalogue, Hatje Cantz 2014, ISBN 978-3-7757-3830-9; ARCH+ 217 (2014). The plan diagram and
   elevation (r1-0003/0004) come from here; the catalogue may hold a measured survey of the pavilion made by CLA/ETH.
9. ARCH+ 252 "Open for Maintenance - Wegen Umbau geoeffnet" (2023), ISBN 978-3-95905-703-5, paid online reader
   (https://archplus.net/de/archiv/english-publication/Open-for-Maintenance/). Features pp. 204-227 on the pavilion.
10. Robert Fleck, Die Biennale von Venedig (standard reference cited by Stylepark).
