# R1 hand list (closed to scripts; for the user's browser): German Pavilion

Save each file into `Projects/Venice Biennale Pavilions/Inbox/` under the name given.

1. https://www.archivportal-d.de/item/RZIC77N4SITD6MPP47FMSQ5QZ4PC3SXD (Anubis bot wall): record of "Zentralblatt der
   Bauverwaltung vereinigt mit Zeitschrift fuer Bauwesen"; see whether the 1938 volume (Jg. 58, Nr. 44, pp. 1192-1194,
   "Das deutsche Kunstausstellungsgebaeude in Venedig") is digitised and linked. Save the pages as
   `de_1938_Zentralblatt_Bauverwaltung_p1192-1194.pdf`.
2. https://ahnp.ub.uni-heidelberg.de/journals/kb/article/view/10836/4695 (Anubis bot wall): M. Zucchi, article on the
   1940 Biennale (kritische berichte). Save the PDF as `de_kb_Zucchi_Biennale1940.pdf` only if it shows a plan or
   photo of the German pavilion.
3. https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutscher_Pavillon_(Venedig) and https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padiglione_della_Germania
   (HTTP 429 to scripts): read the literature and Einzelnachweise lists; nothing to save unless a plan is shown.
4. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21st_Venice_Biennale (HTTP 429): bibliography of 1938 articles (Ruediger, Marchiori).
5. https://www.europeana.eu/item/15514/PI_4538 (robots.txt 403): see what it is (search hit for "Kunstausstellungsgebaeude
   in Venedig"); save the image as `de_europeana_15514_PI_4538.jpg` if it is a drawing or photo of the pavilion.

7. https://archiv.deutscher-pavillon.org/2007/english/geschichte.htm (proxy 502 / connection rejected): the old
   pavilion archive site; look for year pages with plans. Save any plan as `de_archiv-deutscher-pavillon_<year>.<ext>`.
