## Books and publications (in copyright; catalogue / purchase only)
- Bruce Stahl & Shari Stahl Gronwald (with Kim Cross), *The Stahl House: Case Study House #22. The Making of a Modernist
  Icon* (Chronicle Chroma, 2021, 208 pp., ISBN 9781797209432): "blueprints, floor plans, and sketches by Pierre Koenig"
  and Buck Stahl's own schematic model (csh22-alt material). Product page
  https://www.chroniclebooks.com/collections/art-design/products/case-study-house-22 shows only the cover (no preview
  spreads). Chapter "The plan" per a library record (WS8).
- Neil Jackson, *Koenig* (Taschen Basic Art, 2007): CSH 22 chapter with plan and sepia perspectives (r3-0030; preview
  spreads r3-0031..0033). Probable source of the sepia drawings re-posted on the web; Spanish edition probable for
  the WikiArquitectura scans.
- Elizabeth A. T. Smith, *Case Study Houses* (Taschen 2002), pp. 300-319 "int. and ext. photos, plans, drawings" per
  PCAD publication 3972 (https://pcad.lib.washington.edu/publication/3972, robots disallows ClaudeBot; not opened).
- *Blueprints for Modern Living* (MOCA/MIT Press 1989) cited by archINFORM 9340 (robots disallows ClaudeBot).
