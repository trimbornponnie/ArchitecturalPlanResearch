# R1 leads

Leads found by route R1 (magazines and books). None of these can be downloaded by a script under the rules.

## Books on archive.org that are lending (borrow) items only
- Esther McCoy, Modern California Houses: Case Study Houses 1945-1962 (Reinhold 1962): lending copy `https://archive.org/details/moderncalifornia0000esth`. The open community copy (row r1-0026) has Google-scan pages; a clean scan from a library would be better. CSH 22 printed pp. 131-142 (plan p. 132, details pp. 134 and 136).
- Neil Jackson, Pierre Koenig 1925-2004: Living with Steel (Taschen 2007): `https://archive.org/details/pierrekoenig19250000jack` (borrow only). Likely plans and sketches of CSH 22.
- Edward R. Ford, The Details of Modern Architecture, vol. 2 (MIT Press, 1996; archive.org item says 1990): `https://archive.org/details/detailsofmoderna0002ford` (borrow only). Fig. 7.40 'Case Study House No. 22, Pierre Koenig, Los Angeles, 1959-1960': probably redrawn construction details (axonometric). High value for details.
- Dominique Rouillard, Building the Slope: Hillside Houses 1920-1960 (1987): `https://archive.org/details/buildingslopehil0000roui` (borrow only); CSH 22 discussed, may have plan/section.
- James Steele, Los Angeles Architecture: the Contemporary Condition (Phaidon 1993): `https://archive.org/details/losangelesarchit0000stee` (FTS hit: 'firescreen detail p. 54, Pierre Koenig: Case Study House #22 p. 55'); probably borrow only, not checked.
- Contemporary Architectural Drawings: Donations to the Avery (Columbia, 1991): `https://archive.org/details/contemporaryarch0000aver` (FTS hit: 'PIERRE KOENIG Los Angeles (b. 1925) A Case Study House #22, Los Angeles, 1959-60 Black ...'). Points to an original Koenig CSH 22 drawing in Avery Architectural & Fine Arts Library, Drawings & Archives, Columbia University (route: Avery Drawings & Archives catalogue / reference desk; ask for the Koenig CSH 22 drawing donated c. 1990). Pass to R2.
- Not found as open copies: Elizabeth A. T. Smith, Blueprints for Modern Living (MOCA 1989); Steele & Jenkins, Pierre Koenig (Phaidon 1998); Smith / Goessel, Case Study Houses (Taschen); Neil Jackson, Pierre Koenig: A View from the Archive (Getty 2009, R2 checks the Getty Virtual Library); Bruce Stahl & Shari Stahl Gronwald, The Stahl House (Chronicle 2021, 'with blueprints and sketches'): catalogue / purchase only.
