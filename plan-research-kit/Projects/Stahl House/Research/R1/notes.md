## Findings

- A&A published CSH 22 four times: May 1959 design announcement (3 exterior perspectives, 1 interior perspective, plan), Oct 1959 progress report (plan + construction photos), Feb 1960 construction report (photos only), May 1960 public-showing notice (location map), June 1960 finished house (plan + Shulman photos). The plan is the same drawing in all three (pool 'future pool' in 1959).
- A&A never printed a section, elevation or detail of CSH 22 in these issues. The only details found are in McCoy 1962 (column base, sliding door, fireplace veneer; plus a pencil-annotated plan print).
- Best copies: USModernist A&A issues (3490x4372 px pages). McCoy pages max 2040 px wide.
- Photo sources (no rows): A&A Feb 1960 p. 26 (construction), A&A Jun 1960 pp. 14-21 (Shulman), House & Home Aug 1960 p. 147, McCoy 1962 pp. 131-142.
