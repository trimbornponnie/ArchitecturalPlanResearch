# R1 hand list (for a person in a browser)

Nothing from route R1 needs a person in a browser: every source R1 used answered the guard. Items closed to scripts are borrow-only books, listed in leads.md (they need a library loan, not a browser save).

If the user wants a clean copy of the McCoy plates without Google marks, a person with an Internet Archive account could borrow `https://archive.org/details/moderncalifornia0000esth` and look at printed pp. 131-142, but saving borrowed pages is not allowed; use a library copy instead.
