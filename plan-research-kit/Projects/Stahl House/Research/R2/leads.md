# R2 leads

## Getty Research Institute, Pierre Koenig papers and drawings, 1925-2007, accession 2006.M.30

- Finding aid: https://oac.cdlib.org/findaid/ark:/13030/kt4h4nf4b3/entire_text/ (Ann Harrison, 2010; 239.6 linear
  ft). OAC sits behind an AWS WAF: read in a browser. Reference/reproduction requests via the GRI Library "Ask a
  reference question" form, http://hdl.handle.net/10020/askref (a person must send it; not done here).
- CSH 22 containers per the upstream research (2026-09-26; not re-read in this run because OAC and Wayback were not
  reachable to scripts): flatfiles 20, 21, 22, 22D (drawings); 22A-22C (4 exhibition originals); box 1A folders 1-4;
  box 61* folders 5-6; boxes 45-46 (engineering / structural calculations).
- What to ask for: scans of the working drawings set (plans, sections, elevations, framing and steel details), the
  structural drawings and calculations in boxes 45-46 (engineer's name), the exhibition presentation drawings
  (22A-22C, probably the perspectives), and any preliminary schemes (csh22-alt).
- No digitised CSH 22 drawing was found on getty.edu (searches 4, 6, 8). The Getty CDWA example drawing is CSH 21
  (other building).

## Los Angeles Historic-Cultural Monument No. 670 file (designated 1999-11-09)

- City of Los Angeles, Department of City Planning, Office of Historic Resources (OHR), 221 N. Figueroa St.;
  https://planning.lacity.gov/preservation-design/historic-resources . The 1999 Cultural Heritage Commission case
  file (application, staff report, photos; possibly a site plan or plans supplied by the owners) is not online as
  far as found (searches 2-3). A person can ask OHR for the HCM 670 file. The CHC resolution supporting the 2013
  NRHP listing is reproduced on NRHP nomination p.28 (r2-0001).
- Mills Act contract (mentioned on Wikipedia): Mills Act applications in LA include a 10-year maintenance plan and
  sometimes plans; ask OHR together with the HCM file.
- HistoricPlacesLA (http://historicplacesla.org, the LA city inventory/SurveyLA portal): TLS name mismatch to
  scripts; open in a browser and search "1635 Woods" for the HCM record (expect text and photos only).

## LADBS building records for 1635 Woods Drive, Los Angeles 90069 (APN 5556-031-003)

- LADBS Building Records (permits, certificates of occupancy, and microfilmed plans when kept):
  https://ladbsdoc.lacity.org/ (address search form: a person must use it; not used here). Original 1959 permit
  and any later permits (pool, repairs). Plans on microfilm are released only with the owner's / architect's
  consent under California Health & Safety Code 19851; ask LADBS Records counter. Lead only.
- ZIMAS (http://zimas.lacity.org) gives the parcel profile (HCM 670, zoning, tract 13044?) - browser only.

## Getty Research Institute: high-resolution scan of the CSH 22 perspective (r2-0005)

  csh22-alt, location unknown (family).
