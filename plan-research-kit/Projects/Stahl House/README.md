# Stahl House (Case Study House No. 22): drawings

Pierre Koenig, Case Study House No. 22 for Arts & Architecture, built for C. H. "Buck" and Carlotta Stahl,
1635 Woods Drive, Los Angeles. Designed 1957-59, built 1959-60. William Porush was the consulting engineer and Robert
Brady the general contractor (A&A May 1960, p. 26, row r1-0015; `confirmed`). It is LA Historic-Cultural Monument
No. 670 (1999) and on the National Register (ref. 13000519, 2013).

Research run of 2026-10-10. Routes: R1 magazines and books, R2 official and institutional records, R3 web
publications, CAD and 3D, R4 orchestrator gap fix. Every request went through `tools/guard.py`; the request log is
`_work/guard/requests.jsonl`.

## Result in one paragraph

Every drawing of CSH 22 that a script may reach is downloaded: 32 sheets in `Plan set/`, cut from or linked to
28 files in `Downloads/` (244 MB). The best material is Koenig's own presentation drawings as Arts & Architecture
printed them in May 1959, Oct 1959 and June 1960. USModernist scanned those issues at 3490x4372 px per page, so the
drawings are 1000-3000 px. Esther McCoy's 1962 book adds a plan print with Koenig's pencil notes and three
construction details. **No working drawings, elevations, framing plans or measured survey of CSH 22 are online.**
They are in the Pierre Koenig papers at the Getty Research Institute (2006.M.30) and are not digitised. Their
containers are listed under "Reproduction requests" below. Sections exist only as a modern redraw at 849 px
(sheets 027 and 031).

Notes on the set:
- A&A printed no section, elevation or detail of CSH 22. Sheets 005 and 007 show the same plan drawing; so do the
  web copies 018-020, 026 and 030. Sheet 008 (McCoy) is a different print with handwritten notes.
- Sheets 013-032 are re-posts or redraws with no licence stated and are under 2000 px on the long side. Use them
  for orientation only, not for measurement.
- The whole issues stay in `Downloads/` as sources: USModernist A&A 1959-05, 1959-10, 1960-05, 1960-06; the
  archive.org microfilm copies of 1959-05, 1959-10, 1960-06 (lower contrast, kept as second copies); the A&A
  excerpt PDF `artsandarchitecture.com/22.pdf` (the finished-house article, 8 pages, about 1435x1868 px); the final
  NRHP nomination (`npgallery.nps.gov/r2-0001.pdf`, 28 pages, no plan, section or elevation); and the student deck.
- **Decision for you:** the McCoy PDF (sheets 008-011) is an archive.org community upload of a scan that carries
  "Digitized by Google / Original from University of Michigan" marks. It is not a Google Books download, but the
  briefing forbids "Google Books page images". Delete rows r1-0026..0030 from the project (`exclude_rows` in
  `project.json`) if you read the rule as covering this file.

## Rule log of the run (summary; details in each route's notes.md)

- No request outside the guard, and no WebFetch in any route.
- R1: a shared helper script in the scratchpad wrote two of R1's rows into R2's manifest (r2-0003, r2-0004). They
  stay in R2's file as written and are excluded in `project.json` as duplicates of r1-0003 and r1-0004.
- R2: at 15:12 it chained three gets with `;` instead of a stop-on-error loop. The MoMA get ran after a 404 and drew
  a 403, which closed www.moma.org for the project. R2 also made two judgment calls: one request to sirismm.si.edu
  (it answered 526) and one to www.historicplacesla.org after the bare domain failed on TLS.
- R3: upload.wikimedia.org answered 429. The guard's User-Agent carries no contact address, because
  `PLAN_RESEARCH_CONTACT` was left unset on purpose and Wikimedia rate-limits such agents.
- web.archive.org could not be reached: the session's network proxy closed the tunnel both times. To use Wayback
  copies in a later run, add web.archive.org to the cloud environment's allowed domains.
- Hosts refused for the project are in `_work/guard/refused_hosts.json`.
